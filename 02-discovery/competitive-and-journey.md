# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A delivery driver who relies on the RouteLogic mobile app to navigate routes, complete deliveries and record proof of delivery.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To complete deliveries efficiently, with reliable navigation, up-to-date routes and minimal interruptions.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** The app crashes mid-route when the stop list exceeds approximately 40 stops, causing the driver to lose their remaining route and reload it. Poor connectivity can also leave the driver with a blank route, while completing a delivery requires navigating three screens.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** WhatsApp, text messages, phone calls to dispatch, screenshots of routes and paper delivery manifests.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Take screenshots or keep a paper copy of the delivery route.

Attempt to use RouteLogic to complete deliveries.

When the app crashes or fails, contact dispatch by phone or text.

Retrieve missing delivery information from dispatch or backup records.

Continue deliveries using the recovered information.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Drivers cannot rely on RouteLogic to complete deliveries without interruptions. When the app crashes, loses routes or makes simple tasks unnecessarily complicated, drivers waste time and must rely on manual workarounds.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** UXR-01 (Diego): Texts his dispatcher instead of navigating three screens to mark a delivery complete.

UXR-03 (Elena): Lost her remaining stops after an app crash and spent 20 minutes recovering them by phone.

UXR-06 (Priti): Takes daily screenshots because offline routes do not load.

UXR-12 (Focus group): Five of seven drivers keep paper manifests as backups.

BUG-2031, BUG-2050 and BUG-2055: Confirm route crashes, offline failures and complicated delivery completion.
