# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Delivery drivers (primary persona) and fleet dispatch coordinators (secondary persona) who need to complete daily logistics tasks efficiently without complicated workflows or unreliable systems.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** Percentage of dispatch and compliance workflows completed successfully without manual workarounds.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Users encounter complicated workflows, system crashes and unreliable route information, forcing them to rely on phone calls, screenshots, paper records and other manual workarounds to complete essential tasks.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Maintain dispatch reliability and compliance accuracy at their existing baseline while simplifying workflows.

## Scan the backlog & set a human baseline
- **My instinctive “quick wins” before touching the AI (2 to 3 feature IDs + why):** Driver alert notifications. Push alerts for route or job changes and compliance flags, so everyone acts on real-time information instead of finding out late.

Step progress indicator. Shows where each driver is in the dispatch flow, so the team knows what’s happening and can contact a driver at the right moment.

Smart daily report (AI auto-filled). Pulls in the day’s activity automatically, so drivers don’t have to click through several screens to review or submit. Managers see trends, what’s working, and problems early enough to fix them fast.

## Audit, override & decide
- **Where did you override the AI? (feature + old vs. new score + why):** I overrode the AI's initial prioritisation by giving greater importance to Smart Daily Report Auto-Fill (B2) and Driver Alert Notifications (B6).

The AI initially rated B2 as a major project because of its implementation complexity. However, my M2/M3 research highlighted fragmented workflows, excessive manual reporting and delays. I therefore prioritised B2 because automating daily reports could reduce repetitive tasks and improve operational visibility.

I also elevated B6 because timely route changes and compliance alerts could prevent avoidable delays and help coordinators respond proactively. Although the AI assigned it a relatively low value, my assessment considered its operational importance.

I retained the One-Click Compliance Checklist as a high priority because it directly addresses the 14.6-minute compliance bottleneck. I also selected the Step Progress Indicator to improve workflow visibility.
- **Did the AI over-value a Sales/Eng request your M2 interviews don’t support?:** Yes. The AI over-valued the Fleet Analytics Manager View (B8), a Sales-driven request. My M2 research highlighted operational challenges such as lengthy compliance checks, fragmented workflows and manual workarounds rather than a need for executive analytics. I therefore deprioritised B8 in favour of features that directly address users' everyday problems.
- **Did it underweight something your M3 cohort/funnel data strongly supports?:** Yes. The AI undervalued the Step Progress Indicator (B5). My M3 funnel analysis highlighted friction in completing dispatch workflows, making progress visibility important. I prioritised B5 because it helps users understand their current stage, reduces uncertainty and supports workflow completion. I also elevated Smart Daily Report Auto-Fill (B2) because reducing manual steps could improve efficiency and completion rates.

## Generate your interactive roadmap
- **My “Now” lane (this sprint), the 2 to 3 quick wins I’ll build first:** One-Click Compliance Checklist (B1): Simplifies the 14.6-minute compliance process, reduces manual effort and ensures drivers meet compliance requirements before dispatch.

Step Progress Indicator (B5): Improves visibility across the dispatch workflow, helping users track progress and identify incomplete steps.

Smart Daily Report Auto-Fill (B2): Reduces repetitive reporting tasks, streamlines submissions and improves operational visibility.
- **What I cut, and the “no” I’m protecting the scope from:** I deprioritised Fleet Analytics Manager View (B8) and Contextual AI ETA Display (B7) because they require significant effort without directly addressing the primary compliance bottleneck.

I also deferred the full Mobile-First Coordinator Dashboard (B4) because its complexity exceeds our four-week pilot capacity.

Driver Alert Notifications (B6) remains a Next priority, as timely alerts are valuable but less directly connected to the primary success metric.

My decision protects the team from scope creep, particularly Sales-driven requests and complex features that could delay delivery of measurable improvements to compliance workflows.
