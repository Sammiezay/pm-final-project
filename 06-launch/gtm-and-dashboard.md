# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6)

## Get your prior work ready
- **The feature you're launching from your M4 PRD, one feature, not a list:** The Smart Daily Report feature for RouteLogic, which automatically pre-fills daily operational reports to reduce manual reporting time, improve accuracy and help dispatch managers complete their reporting tasks more efficiently.
- **What your M5 experiment told you shipped / iterating, the evidence behind the launch:** The experiment evaluated whether the Smart Daily Report feature could reduce report completion time compared with the existing process. The baseline was 14.6 minutes, with a proposed 30% improvement target. The launch decision will depend on whether the experiment achieves the required improvement without compromising reporting accuracy.
- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** A dispatch manager at a logistics company who coordinates daily driver activities, monitors operational progress and prepares reports. They need accurate, timely information but face challenges with repetitive manual reporting, fragmented information and time-consuming administrative tasks.

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Drive active adoption and regular use of the Smart Daily Report feature among existing RouteLogic dispatch managers.
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** The feature is being introduced to existing RouteLogic users, so the priority is encouraging them to use it regularly rather than building awareness. Increasing engagement will help validate whether the feature reduces manual reporting time, improves efficiency and becomes part of dispatch managers' daily workflows.
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Existing RouteLogic dispatch managers at small and medium-sized logistics companies who prepare daily operational reports, manage multiple drivers and spend significant time manually collecting and entering reporting information.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M - Targeted Launch
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** The feature targets existing RouteLogic dispatch managers and has the potential to improve operational efficiency by reducing manual reporting time. Although there is no immediate direct revenue impact, increased adoption could improve customer satisfaction and retention. Without targeted communication, users may overlook the feature, limiting adoption and its potential value.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** This is a launch, not just a release. The feature requires targeted communication, user onboarding and guidance to encourage adoption. Simply making it available may not change existing reporting habits. A focused go-to-market approach will help dispatch managers understand its benefits and incorporate it into their daily workflows.

## Choose your top three channels and plan assets
- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Channel 1 – In-App Notifications (Owned)
Use in-app banners and guided prompts to introduce the Smart Daily Report feature to existing dispatch managers. This reaches active users directly within their workflow and encourages immediate adoption.
- **Channel 2 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Channel 2 – Email Campaign (Owned)
Send targeted emails to existing dispatch managers highlighting the feature's benefits, including reduced reporting time and improved efficiency. Include a short demonstration and a clear call to action to encourage usage.
- **Channel 3 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Channel 3 – Customer Success Webinars (Owned)
Host short, interactive demonstrations for dispatch managers and operations leads. These sessions will explain how the feature works, answer questions and help users incorporate it into their daily reporting processes.
- **Enablement & assets what Sales / CS / Support need, plus the assets to build (one-pager, demo, etc.):** Equip Sales, Customer Success and Support teams with a feature overview, key benefits, demo video, FAQs, onboarding guide and troubleshooting resources. Sales will communicate the business value, Customer Success will drive adoption, and Support will resolve user issues. All teams will use consistent messaging focused on reducing manual reporting time and improving efficiency.

## Make it executable
- **Ownership named owner per key activity, individual, not department:** Product Manager (GTM lead): Overall launch coordination and success metrics. Marketing Lead: Email campaigns and in-app messaging. Customer Success Manager: Webinars, onboarding and adoption. Support Lead: FAQs and troubleshooting. Engineering Lead: Feature readiness and technical issues.
- **Budget & resource gaps what costs extra, and any asset you can't currently build:** The launch will primarily use existing resources and owned channels, keeping additional costs low. Potential expenses include demo video production, webinar tools and onboarding materials. Resource gaps may include design support, content creation and Customer Success capacity. These should be confirmed before launch.
- **Timeline Phase 1 beta → Phase 2 launch moment → Phase 3 post-launch:** Phase 1 – Beta (Weeks 1–2): Test with selected dispatch managers, gather feedback, validate reporting accuracy and resolve usability issues.

Phase 2 – Launch (Week 3): Release to the target audience, activate in-app notifications, send targeted emails and deliver onboarding webinars.

Phase 3 – Post-Launch (Weeks 4–6): Monitor feature adoption, reporting completion time and user feedback. Address issues and prioritise improvements.

## Define how you'll know it worked
- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** Feature Adoption Rate: Target 60% of eligible dispatch managers using Smart Daily Report within 30 days.

Weekly Active Usage: Target 50% of eligible dispatch managers using the feature at least once per week.

Reporting Efficiency: Reduce average report completion time by 30%, from 14.6 minutes to approximately 10.2 minutes.
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** High initial adoption but low repeat usage would indicate that users are trying the feature but not finding sustained value. Another warning sign would be reporting time decreasing while errors or manual corrections increase, suggesting that efficiency is compromising accuracy.
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** If adoption or repeat usage falls below target after 30 days, investigate user feedback and improve usability, onboarding or feature functionality. If engagement targets are met and reporting accuracy remains stable, expand the rollout to more users.
